# NEWMODEL — Research Protocol
Version: 1.0
Status: ACCEPTED AS WORKING METHOD

## Purpose
Prevent research from becoming unsupported narrative.

## Evidence classes
### SOURCE
Directly supported by a cited/accessible source.

### INFERENCE
Reasoned conclusion derived from available evidence.

### PROPOSAL
A recommended direction requiring product acceptance.

### ASSUMPTION
A temporary statement required to continue.

### UNKNOWN
Not established.

## Research rules
- Never invent data.
- Preserve source terminology when the source is the requested basis.
- Distinguish source facts from model inference.
- Record date for time-sensitive information.
- Prefer primary/official sources where practical.
- Do not treat one public signal as proof of a systemic condition.
- If evidence is insufficient, say so.
- For enterprise-system research, verify actual configured data relationships before designing around them.

## Research output
A useful research artifact should normally contain:
1. Question
2. Scope
3. Sources
4. Findings
5. Evidence
6. Inferences
7. Risks
8. Unknowns
9. Implications
10. Recommendation / proposal
11. Next validation step
