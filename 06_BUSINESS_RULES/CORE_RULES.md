# NEWMODEL — Core Business Rules
Version: 0.1
Status: PROPOSED — REQUIRES USER GATE

## Rule R-001 — Evidence classification
Every material value must be classified when relevant as FACT, ASSUMPTION, FORECAST, OPTION, DECISION or ACTUAL.

## Rule R-002 — Source traceability
Material externally sourced information must retain source/reference and timestamp where available.

## Rule R-003 — Feasibility gate
An option with unresolved mandatory feasibility conditions cannot be presented as fully executable.

## Rule R-004 — Human accountability
System recommendation does not equal management decision.

## Rule R-005 — No silent override
A user or automation must not silently override a material constraint without recording the action and authority.

## Rule R-006 — Decision deadline
A time-critical decision should expose its decision deadline and consequences of lateness.

## Rule R-007 — Decision outcome
A completed decision should be eligible for outcome capture and deviation analysis.

## Rule R-008 — External-system authority
When a specialist system is the declared source of a capability, NEWMODEL should not silently replace its result with internally generated data.

## Rule R-009 — Data freshness
Stale evidence should be visible where freshness can affect decision quality.

## Rule R-010 — Missing evidence
Missing critical evidence should be visible rather than silently inferred.

## Rule R-011 — Architecture integrity
Business rules must not be embedded solely in UI behavior when they require reuse, audit or testing.

## Rule R-012 — Auditability
Material decision changes must be attributable to actor, time and reason.
